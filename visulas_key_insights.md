# Insights from LinkedIn Jobs Data 📊

- Most candidates preferred applying through offsite platforms, showing that online job applications are faster and more convenient.

- Remote work opportunities were lower in 2024, especially for mid-senior level roles. However, the highest number of applications were still submitted for mid-senior positions, followed by entry-level and fresher roles.

- Full-time jobs received the highest number of applications, followed by contract and part-time roles. Salary trends followed a similar pattern, with full-time roles offering the highest compensation.

- Peak job posting times were observed between 10:30 PM to 11:00 PM, followed by around 5:00 PM, 8:40 PM, and 12:20 AM to 12:45 AM.

- Most applicants preferred jobs offering yearly salary packages, while hourly pay was more common in full-time job applications.
